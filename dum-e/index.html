About

DUM-E

My Claude Code agent harness. Six agent sessions take a card from a frozen spec to checked code:

  1. The coordinator freezes the spec and splits it into sub-tasks.
  2. The builder works each one, with at most 3 tries.
  3. A separate verifier checks the result against the spec.
  4. A miss goes back for a new plan, not a blind retry. After 2 replans, a person decides.
  5. The learner turns every miss into a lesson for next time.

This is the desk I watch it on. Press Replay to watch card SH-2 fail verification twice, get a new plan, then pass.
